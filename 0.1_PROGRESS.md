## 7. Accepted architectural decisions

Modular mini-domain libraries, infrastructure-independent domains, `ClientEngine` as single writer, parameterized SQL files, direct work on `main`, device-owned data, ephemeral untrusted relay and direct Tor messaging remain mandatory.

## 8. Blockers and risks

The preparation environment did not provide `cargo`, `rustc`, `flutter` or `pwsh`. GitHub Actions must validate compilation, analysis and tests. Keep `torca-foundation` small, keep workflow state out of Flutter, and do not let CI diverge from `scripts/validate.ps1`.
